I'm Beatty Fulmina (he/they), an art generalist with experience in using a range of 2D and 3D processes to create animations, games, and illustrations.


The process of creation is as important to me as the final products, so I love to seek out new opportunities and projects to accumulate more skills!


I do not use, nor consent, to my work being used to train AI.
