There's a legend that, deep down into the open maw of the forest, your wishes will be granted by one of the fair folk.

What is freely given may only be borrowed, however, lest the fae come collect more than you bargained for...

And to those who belong to the forest and to the forest must return, fate will take them back.

One way or another. Whether they wished for it or not.
